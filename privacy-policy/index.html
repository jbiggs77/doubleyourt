Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DoubleYourT.com (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and shares information when you visit our website. If you have questions, contact privacy@doubleyourt.com.

1. Information we collect

a. Information you provide directly

If you subscribe to our newsletter or email us, we receive the email address and any details you include in your message. We do not ask for sensitive personal information, and you should not send it.

b. Information collected automatically

When you visit, we and our analytics and tracking providers may automatically record your IP address, browser user agent, referring page, the pages and links you interact with, and timestamps. We use this to measure which content and links our readers actually engage with.

c. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners may use cookies, pixels and similar technologies to measure traffic and attribute clicks and purchases to the site that referred them. Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies in their settings; the site remains usable if you do.

d. Information from partners and affiliates

Merchants and affiliate networks may tell us that a click we referred resulted in a purchase, and the value of that purchase, so we can be paid. We receive this as commission reporting.

2. How we use information

  • To operate, maintain and improve the website and its content.
  • To understand which guides and products readers find useful.
  • To attribute referrals and receive commissions from partners.
  • To send our newsletter, if you asked for it.
  • To detect and prevent fraud, abuse and automated traffic.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information for money. We share information with:

  • Service providers who host the site, deliver email, and provide analytics on our behalf.
  • Advertising and affiliate partners, who receive click and conversion data needed to attribute and pay commissions.
  • Legal recipients, where required by law or to protect our rights.

Some jurisdictions define “sale” or “sharing” broadly enough to include advertising-related transfers like these. If you want to opt out, email privacy@doubleyourt.com and we will honor it.

4. Third-party websites

Our links take you to sites we do not control. This policy does not apply to them. Read their privacy policies before sharing information or making a purchase.

5. Data retention

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, to meet legal and accounting obligations, and to resolve disputes. Newsletter subscriptions are kept until you unsubscribe.

6. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of targeted advertising or the sale/sharing of personal information. To exercise any of these, email privacy@doubleyourt.com with the subject “Privacy Request” and tell us your state or country of residence. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. You can unsubscribe from email at any time using the link in any message.

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children’s privacy

This site is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International visitors

We operate in the United States. If you visit from elsewhere, your information will be processed in the United States, where privacy laws may differ from those in your country.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “last updated” date above reflects the current version.
