About

Our purpose and how we operate

Last updated: August 29, 2026

DoubleYourT.com is an independent information and comparison website covering telehealth companies and other providers that offer testosterone therapy and related men's health services. We are not a medical provider, pharmacy, or telehealth company.

What we do

  • Compare providers on price, availability, and how the service works.
  • Explain options in plain language so you can prepare questions for a licensed clinician.
  • Link you to providers so you can confirm current pricing and eligibility yourself.

How we choose what to feature

Our guides are produced by the DoubleYourT.com editorial team. We publish our method so you can judge the recommendations for yourself:

  • We compare providers on publicly available information about services, pricing and availability.
  • We weigh practical factors — convenience, what is included, and the support options offered.
  • Compensation from partners can influence placement and the order in which companies appear.
  • We do not provide medical care, diagnosis, treatment or prescriptions, and nothing here is medical advice.
  • Treatment decisions belong between you and a licensed healthcare professional.

How we keep the lights on

We may earn commissions when you buy through our links, and we work with sponsors. That compensation can influence which products appear and in what order. Full details are in our Advertising Disclosure.
